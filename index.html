How it works

Ricardo's league, Ricardo's rules. Here they are in short form.

1

Point a game

Pick the straight-up winner of every game on the slate. No point spreads, no half-measures. Each correct pick is one point.

3

Primetime

Sunday night and Monday night games are worth three. They are the games everybody actually watches, so they are the games that swing the table.

+3

Perfect week

Run the table — every game on the slate, primetime included — and take three bonus points on top. Week 1 is sixteen games, so a clean sweep is twenty-three.

0

If you forget

No picks, no points. Ricardo can see the timestamp on your submission, so a pick that lands after kickoff does not count.

The deadline

There is no single cutoff for the week. Every game closes at its own kickoff, and nothing else. Get the whole slate in by Thursday if you want it off your plate — or wait on the injury report and send Sunday's games Sunday morning and the Monday nighter Monday morning.

Miss a kickoff and you lose that game only. The rest of your week is still live. And you can change a pick as many times as you like until its game starts — the last slip in before kickoff is the one that counts. No spreads means no reason to lock you in early.

The prize

To be determined, and determined by Ricardo. Whoever is on top when the regular season ends takes it.

A hint from the commissioner

“Even a quick conversation with ChatGPT on a Thursday morning can help you earn points.” Consider yourself advised.
